139,321 records ingested via bulk COPY in 1.87s, with B-tree indexes on location, cuisine, rating, cost, and veg-status. Queries use window functions, CTEs, and BCG-style quadrant classification.

Key Business Insights (1/2)

🎯 Pricing Sweet Spot — ₹401–₹700: Highest satisfaction (4.16⭐) and nearly 2× the reviews (948 vs 481) vs. budget tier, despite budget (<₹200) dominating volume.
🍲 Specialist beats Generalist: Single-cuisine kitchens score higher (4.09⭐ vs 4.02⭐) and earn 13.2% more reviews than 5+ cuisine menus.
🏷️ Promotional Lift: 3+ active offers drive a 45.6% review surge (581 vs 399) with no rating penalty.

Key Business Insights (2/2)

🥗 Pure-Veg Whitespace: Ahmedabad (77%), Indore (74%), Surat (73.7%) are veg-dominant; Kolkata & Goa (18%) are meat-dominant — a clear cloud-kitchen targeting signal.
⚠️ Cold-Start Deficit: 52.2% of Jaipur and 46.0% of Agra restaurants have zero reviews — a gap for "First-Order Review" cashback campaigns.

Dashboard & Tech Stack

Power BI dashboard styled in Swiggy's Deep Navy & Vibrant Orange brand theme, with KPI trend cards, multi-select city search, dietary filters, price-band slicers, and a national-chains leaderboard (Domino's, KFC, Pizza Hut & more). A live HTML prototype is also included in the repo.
